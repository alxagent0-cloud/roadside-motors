FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you transport vehicles that don't drive?

Yes — non-drivable vehicles are a core part of our work. We need to know whether it rolls, whether it steers, what type of damage it has, and we ask for photos so we can plan the loading correctly.

What if the vehicle is locked or the wheels are blocked?

Tell us in the request. Locked vehicles and blocked wheels change how we load, and we bring the right equipment — but only if we know in advance.

Do you operate nationwide or only locally?

Nationwide across Germany and across Europe. Longer routes and exports are quoted individually.

Do you buy damaged vehicles?

Yes. Accident, hail, engine and gearbox damage, vehicles without TÜV, high mileage and export vehicles. Send the details and photos and we'll come back with an offer.

Can you repair my vehicle, or do you only transport it?

We coordinate repairs — accident repair, bodywork, painting, hail damage and wheel refurbishment — through our specialist partners. You keep one contact with us and we maintain quality control over the whole process.

Do you source parts and tyres?

We do, on request. Send the vehicle or camper details, the part you need and photos if you have them. We check availability and price and get back to you.
